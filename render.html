Six stories that shaped the day, around the globe
ASIA-PACIFIC · SUMMITS

Leaders gather in Shenzhen as APEC opens

The APEC summit opened in Shenzhen for two days of talks. Donald Trump arrived in Manila for Quad discussions, and a first three-way meeting of Xi Jinping, Trump and Vladimir Putin is set for Thursday in Shenzhen.

MIDDLE EAST · CONFLICT

US strike on Iran base as attacks trade across the Gulf

The United States confirmed a strike on an Iranian Revolutionary Guard missile base on Qeshm Island, described as self-defence. Iran and the US traded attacks on Qeshm and Bahrain, Kuwait reported drone attacks and interceptions, and Bahrain later announced an all-clear.

KOREAN PENINSULA · SECURITY

North Korea fires missile after allied drills

North Korea launched a ballistic missile towards the Sea of Japan, hours after joint military drills by the United States, South Korea and Japan. The launch added fresh tension to a region already on edge.

EUROPE · WAR

Russia and Ukraine trade drone strikes despite peace talks

Russia and Ukraine exchanged drone attacks, even as peace talks continued in Abu Dhabi. The fighting showed no sign of easing while diplomats kept working on a settlement.

EUROPE · DISASTER

5.3 magnitude earthquake shakes Romania

A 5.3 magnitude earthquake struck Romania, rattling buildings and alarming residents. The tremor was felt across the region, and authorities checked for damage and injuries.

SPACE · SCIENCE

Voyager 1 nears one light-day from Earth

NASA’s Voyager 1 is about to become the first human-made object to reach one light-day from Earth, nearly 26 billion kilometres away. Launched in 1977, the probe keeps sending data from interstellar space.
